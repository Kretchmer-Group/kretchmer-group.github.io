## Victor Suarez

Victor received his B.S. in Chemistry from the University of Florida in 2021. His current research interests include methodological extensions to ring-polymer molecular dynamics for describing nonadiabatic reactions, such as condensed-phase electron transfer and photoinduced chemical reactions. His more recent work focuses on extending real-time electronic dynamics methodologies to account for nonradiative electronic decay processes, such as Auger–Meitner decay and intermolecular Coulombic decay.
